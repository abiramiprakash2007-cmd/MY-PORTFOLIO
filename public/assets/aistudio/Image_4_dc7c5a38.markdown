## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intersection of advanced artificial intelligence and elevated personal identity. Tailored for an ambitious AI Engineer and B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) scholar, the aesthetic merges technical rigor with delicate, avant-garde grace. The brand voice is intellectually authoritative, visionary, and immaculately polished.

The visual direction combines **Ethereal Glassmorphism** with **Neo-Technical Minimalism**. It deliberately moves away from cold, hyper-masculine cybersecurity tropes or raw terminal brutality. Instead, it frames cutting-edge computation—neural networks, deep learning models, and algorithmic research—through soft ambient light, crystalline frosted panels, precise hair-line structural borders, and deep cosmic contrast. 

The emotional impact must inspire awe, intellectual trust, and effortless refinement, communicating both technical depth and creative distinction to recruiters, researchers, and venture founders.

## Colors

The palette operates in a default high-depth dark mode, utilizing deep midnight tones as a canvas for soft prismatic radiation.

### Foundations & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0F19`)**: Deep obsidian-midnight navy. Absorbs light and provides infinite contrast without the stark harshness of pure black.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#111625`)**: Primary container background for underlying layouts.
- **Surface Glass (`rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.45)`)**: Deep twilight navy tint formulated for blurred backdrop layers.
- **Surface Glass Elevated (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`)**: Top-level highlight panels capturing ambient luminance.

### Accent & Radiance Tiers
- **Primary Lavender (`#E0D4FC`)**: The signature intellectual highlight. Applied to primary hero statements, primary interactive elements, and high-impact focal points. Accompanied by whisper-lavender (`#F3EEFF`) for subtle fills and glowing text accents.
- **Pastel Blush Rose (`#FBCFE8` / `#FDE2E4`)**: Humanist counter-accent representing elegance and multidimensionality. Used for research tags, sub-metrics, and secondary graphical motifs.
- **Tertiary Cyber Emerald (`#6EE7B7`)**: Soft neon mint/cyan accent strictly reserved for operational metrics, live deployment indicators, pipeline statuses, and verification badges.
- **Borders & Strokes (`rgba(224, 212, 252, 0.14)`)**: Translucent frosted edges providing crisp structure without breaking atmospheric depth.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances modern geometric proportions with monospaced precision.

- **Primary Typeface (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric clarity with softened apertures, ensuring large headers project confidence without aggressive starkness. Body text retains high legibility across translucent and dark backgrounds.
- **Technical Typeface (`Space Mono`)**: Introduced purposefully for all AI metadata, parameter counts, model architecture tags, timestamps, and metric labels. It grounds abstract engineering feats in precision instrumentation.
- **Hierarchical Discipline**: Body text remains restrained in `#FFFFFF` with muted variants utilizing 70% opacity white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.70)`) to ensure high contrast against dark glass planes.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid constrained by an expansive maximum content container of `1280px` to maintain generous negative space and prevent visual crowding.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `4rem` (64px) outer margin. Asymmetric multi-column layouts accommodate research paper cards, architecture diagrams, and interactive AI showcases.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2.5rem` (40px) outer margin. Heavy grids collapse gracefully into dual-column modules.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) outer canvas margin. Complex data tables and project showcases stack vertically into single-column cards.

Atmospheric whitespace is intentional: section gaps scale from `5rem` to `8rem`, allowing translucent layers and back-glow accents to breathe.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use opaque drop shadows or physical drop-offs. It relies entirely on **Luminescent Glassmorphic Layering** and **Atmospheric Radiance**.

1. **Base Plane**: Deep `#0B0F19` backdrop with low-frequency radial ambient gradients (`rgba(224, 212, 252, 0.08)` blended with `rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.40)`) anchored in the viewport corners.
2. **Glass Level 1 (Default Containers)**: 
   - Fill: `rgba(17, 22, 37, 0.65)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px)`
   - Border: 1px solid `rgba(224, 212, 252, 0.12)`
   - Shadow: `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.36)`
3. **Glass Level 2 (Interactive & Hovered Modules)**:
   - Fill: `rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.55)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(24px)`
   - Border: 1px solid `rgba(224, 212, 252, 0.28)`
   - Outer Glow: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(224, 212, 252, 0.18), 0 12px 40px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`
4. **Hero Elements & Modals**:
   - Edge highlight featuring an inner gradient stroke (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3), rgba(224, 212, 252, 0.05))`) paired with subtle violet-cyan diffuse haloing (`box-shadow: 0 0 60px -10px rgba(110, 231, 183, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The geometric framework uses level `2` roundedness (0.5rem base, scaling to 1rem for standard cards and 1.5rem for primary glass surfaces). 

This curvature softens the technical exactness of AI data dashboards while preserving clean structural geometry. Micro-elements like tag pills and status indicators take on a full capsule shape (`9999px`) to create an intentional visual hierarchy against squarer structural modules. Inner interactive elements mirror outer container radii proportionally to ensure clean optical nesting.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Radiant Button**: Solid lavender background (`#E0D4FC`) with deep navy typography (`#0B0F19`, font weight 600). Features a subtle layered bloom `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(224, 212, 252, 0.35)`. Transitions to crisp white on hover with expanded glow radius.
- **Secondary Glass Button**: Translucent surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) with a 1px border (`rgba(224, 212, 252, 0.20)`), white text, and a soft glow on hover accompanied by a 1px border shift to `#FBCFE8`.
- **Tertiary Minimal Link**: Monospace label with arrow glyph `→`, underlined with a 1px lavender rule that expands dynamically from center-left on hover.

### Badges & Technical Chips
- **AI Model / Tech Tag**: Monospace (`Space Mono`, 11px, all-caps). Surface: `rgba(224, 212, 252, 0.06)`, border: `1px solid rgba(224, 212, 252, 0.18)`, text: `#E0D4FC`. Pill-shaped border radius (`9999px`).
- **Status Indicator**: Features a glowing 6px circular dot (`#6EE7B7` with `box-shadow: 0 0 8px #6EE7B7`) indicating live machine learning endpoints, active research, or availability status.

### Cards & Project Showcase Surfaces
- **Interactive Project Card**: Glass Level 1 foundation with 1rem (`16px`) padding. Inner image/preview container features a subtle 1px border inset. On hover, the border illuminates to lavender/rose gradient opacity and the card elevates by `-4px` Y-translation.
- **Metric / Benchmark Module**: Houses bold numerical metrics (e.g., "99.2% Accuracy", "0.04s Latency") rendered in Plus Jakarta Sans bold with monospace parameter keys directly beneath in muted rose (`#FBCFE8`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Search & Contact Inputs**: Dark glass fills (`rgba(17, 22, 37, 0.70)`), fine-line perimeter borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`). When focused, border switches to `#E0D4FC` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(224, 212, 252, 0.2)`). Placeholder rendered in 40% white.
- **Selection Toggles & Checkboxes**: Custom rounded containers filled with glass twilight navy; checked state reveals an illuminated lavender indicator.

### Research & Code Snippets
- **Model Architecture Card**: Embedded dark panels (`#080B12`) styled as code terminals, featuring delicate frosted header tabs and soft syntax highlighting centered on lavender, blush pink, and mint cyan.
